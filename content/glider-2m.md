## Goal and requirements or constraints
- First major glider project. Built as a hobby, with an engineering approach.
- Built in my bedroom: no composites, no power tools.
- No access to 3D printing or laser cutting at the time.
- Had to be easy to move around the room and fit in a car.

## Design decisions (what, and why)
- Own design, based on my previous models and existing designs.
- Two-part wing: easy to move around the room and fits in the car.
- Airfoil: hand-shaped, small camber, based on previous experience.
- T-tail: simple, and I already had experience with this structure.

## Problems and how I fixed them
- Centre of gravity: tail-heavy. Made a CG balancer (later 3D printed), lightened the tail a lot, added about 50 g of aluminium weights in the nose.
- Electronics: poor quality components. (Details of what failed / how dealt with: later)
- Servo cables in the wing were hard to route. Made a channel (shaft) for the cable, so the servo can be taken out and put back easily; it is not glued in.
